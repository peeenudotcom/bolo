    What can I say for formatting?

    Breaks: new line, next line, line break, skip a line, new paragraph, start a new paragraph.

    Punctuation: comma, period, full stop, question mark, exclamation mark, colon, semicolon, ellipsis, em dash.

    Lists: “bullet point milk, bullet point eggs” or “numbered list milk, next item eggs, end list”.

    Quotes and brackets: open quote / close quote, open parenthesis / close parenthesis, open bracket / close bracket, open brace / close brace.

    Symbols: at sign, underscore, forward slash, hyphen, decimal point, percent sign, plus sign, equals sign, hash sign, ampersand.

    Emoji: heart emoji, thumbs up emoji, folded hands emoji, smile emoji and more.

    With AI cleanup: clear number, currency and time formatting; email addresses; lists; corrections such as “Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday”. Keep names and important numbers in mind when checking the result. These spoken command phrases are in English and can be used within mixed speech.

        How the limit works

        Before each cloud request, Bolo checks that enough budget remains and saves a conservative estimate. Cleanup uses returned token counts when available. Cancelled, failed or interrupted requests keep their reserved estimate because billing is uncertain. This can stop Bolo slightly early. Prices can change; this is a local estimated limit, not a guaranteed account billing cap. Fully offline recognition with AI cleanup off remains available.
